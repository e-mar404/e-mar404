### Week of May 17 - May 23

This week was very busy and did not end up reading or listening to a lot of
things so it should be super short.

## Google I/O

I'll be grouping in a few things here under the title of [Google
I/O](#google-io) since it all relates to that and I have the same thoughts about
it all.

1. 100 things we announced at I/O 2026
    [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/)
2. Everything Google announced at I/O 2026: Gemini, Search, Android XR, & more
    [Article from 9to5Google by Abner Li](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-news/)
3. Google I/O '26 Keynote
    [Live keynote](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYSncx9zLIU)

I tried to watch the live keynote since I have watched WWDC every year for a
while now. I've never really been an apple developer but it does interest me to
see what is available for developers to use in the near future. Now that I have
a google phone I thought that it would be nice to pay attention to what Google 
will be providing developers for the near future.

To put it lightly I did not find the keynote interesting at all. I am now
questioning if I had the purpose of Google I/O all wrong and it is actually not
for developers, and instead is more for the tech enthusiasts that happen to
develop software. Maybe this is what the industry is switching to now,
getting to a place in which we don't need developers any more and just people
that understand problems and are able to prompt just the right way. 

It was ALL AI and agents and how you don't need to know anything anymore. You 
too can can have your own radio show with non-existing scenarios!! [^1]

Long story short, I was not able to finish watching the live keynote due to how
uninteresting it was to me. I had to read the other articles attached above to
get filled in on what was new. And frankly outside of maybe enhancements on 
SynthID[^2], WebMCP[^3], and the universal cart[^4] [^5], there wasn't 
that many interesting new things.
